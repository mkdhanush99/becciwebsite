## Grievance Redressal

BECCI aims to address enquiries, complaints and concerns relating to membership, payments, website information and institutional services.

Individuals may submit grievances by email.

Please include:

- Full name.
- Contact email address.
- Relevant membership or application reference, if applicable.
- Payment transaction ID, if applicable.
- Description of the issue.
- Relevant supporting documents.

BECCI will acknowledge complaints within a reasonable period and endeavour to provide an appropriate response within 15 working days, where practicable.

Matters requiring additional investigation may take longer. The complainant will be informed where further time is required.
