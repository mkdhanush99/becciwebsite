**BECCI Membership and Institutional Service Fees**

BECCI collects membership fees and applicable institutional service fees for membership processing, institutional administration and the services associated with the relevant membership category.

Please read this policy carefully before completing payment.

## 1. Nature of Membership Fees

Membership fees are collected towards institutional membership, administration, processing and the applicable membership-related services.

The applicable fee will be displayed or communicated before the applicant completes payment.

## 2. Non-Refundable Membership Fees

**Membership fees are non-refundable once the membership application has been approved and the membership has been activated.**

Once activated, the membership fee will not ordinarily be refunded if a member:

- Changes their mind after activation.
- Voluntarily decides not to continue.
- Does not use the membership benefits.
- Does not participate in BECCI activities.
- Fails to make use of the available membership services.
- Decides to discontinue membership before the end of its validity period.

Membership fees are collected for institutional membership and associated services, and are not a deposit or investment.

## 3. Applications Not Approved

If an application is rejected before membership activation, any membership fee already collected for that application will be eligible for a refund, subject to verification.

The refund will ordinarily be initiated to the original payment method.

## 4. Duplicate Payments

If an applicant is charged more than once for the same membership transaction, the duplicate payment will be verified and refunded.

## 5. Failed Transactions

If an amount is debited from the applicant's account but BECCI does not receive successful payment confirmation, the applicant should contact BECCI with the transaction reference.

Where the payment is confirmed as unsuccessful or duplicated, the amount will be handled through the payment provider or relevant banking channel.

## 6. Payment Errors

Where a payment has been made for an incorrect amount due to a verified technical or processing error attributable to BECCI or its payment process, BECCI will review the matter and take appropriate corrective action.

## 7. Cancellation Requests

Applicants may request cancellation of a pending membership application before approval and activation.

Cancellation of an application does not automatically create a right to a refund in every circumstance. Any refund will be assessed according to the provisions of this policy and applicable law.

Once membership is approved and activated, voluntary cancellation does not ordinarily entitle the member to a refund.

## 8. Refund Processing Timeline

Approved refunds will ordinarily be initiated within **7–10 working days** after verification and approval.

The actual time taken for the amount to appear in the applicant's account may depend on the payment provider, bank or original payment method.

## 9. How to Request a Refund

Applicants must email:

**[grievance@becci.in](mailto:grievance@becci.in)**

Please provide:

- Applicant's full name.
- Registered email address.
- Membership application or reference number, if available.
- Payment transaction ID.
- Payment date.
- Amount paid.
- Reason for the refund request.
- Relevant payment confirmation or supporting evidence.

Do not send card PINs, UPI PINs, passwords or other confidential payment credentials.

## 10. Applicable Legal Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to restrict any right or remedy available to an applicant or member under applicable Indian law.

BECCI will review genuine payment issues and service-related complaints in accordance with applicable legal requirements.
