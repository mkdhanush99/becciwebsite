## 1. Membership Purpose

BECCI membership is intended to provide an institutional association with the Bahujan Entrepreneurs Chamber for Commerce & Industry and access to the benefits and services applicable to the approved membership category.

## 2. Membership Categories

BECCI may offer different membership categories based on its approved institutional structure.

The applicable category, eligibility, membership duration and fees will be communicated before payment.

## 3. Application and Approval

All membership applications are subject to review and approval by BECCI.

BECCI may request additional information or supporting documents where reasonably required.

Submission of an application does not guarantee acceptance.

## 4. Membership Activation

Membership will be activated only after:

1. The application has been reviewed and approved.
2. The applicable membership fee has been successfully received.
3. The required membership formalities have been completed.

A payment confirmation alone does not constitute membership approval.

## 5. Member Responsibilities

Members are expected to:

- Provide accurate information.
- Maintain professional and ethical conduct.
- Respect BECCI's institutional policies.
- Avoid misrepresenting their membership or affiliation.
- Use membership privileges only for authorised purposes.
- Avoid conduct that may damage BECCI's reputation or the interests of other members.

## 6. Membership Benefits

Benefits may vary according to membership category and applicable institutional programmes.

Membership does not guarantee:

- Business contracts or orders.
- Government grants or subsidies.
- Loans or funding approval.
- Tender awards.
- Employment or business partnerships.
- Guaranteed revenue or commercial returns.

## 7. Membership Renewal

Membership renewal, applicable fees and renewal conditions will be communicated through authorised BECCI channels.

Continued membership is subject to the applicable renewal and institutional requirements.

## 8. Suspension or Termination

BECCI may suspend or terminate membership for material misrepresentation, misuse of institutional identity, serious misconduct or violation of applicable membership conditions.

Where reasonably appropriate, the member may be given an opportunity to respond before a final decision.

## 9. Fees and Refunds

Membership fees are subject to the BECCI [Refund and Cancellation Policy](/refund-policy).

Members are advised to read the Refund and Cancellation Policy before making payment.

## 10. Acceptance

By submitting a membership application and accepting these Terms, the applicant confirms that they have read and understood the applicable membership conditions.
