BECCI respects the privacy of visitors, applicants, members and individuals who communicate with the organisation.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, processed and protected through the BECCI website and its related institutional activities.

## 1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the service used, BECCI may collect:

- Name.
- Email address.
- Mobile number.
- Postal address.
- Business or organisation details.
- Membership category and application details.
- Communication and enquiry information.
- Payment transaction references and payment status.
- Documents voluntarily submitted for a legitimate membership or service purpose.

BECCI aims to collect only information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Respond to enquiries.
- Process membership applications.
- Verify application details.
- Communicate membership decisions.
- Confirm payments.
- Provide relevant institutional services.
- Maintain administrative records.
- Address grievances.
- Meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

## 3. Payment Information

Payments may be processed through authorised third-party payment service providers.

BECCI does not intend to store card PINs, UPI PINs, banking passwords or other confidential payment authentication credentials.

Payment providers process payment information according to their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

## 4. Sharing of Information

BECCI may share relevant information with authorised service providers, payment processors, technology providers or competent authorities where necessary for the purposes described in this Policy or where legally required.

BECCI does not authorise the sale of personal information as a commercial mailing list.

## 5. Data Security

BECCI will take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, disclosure or loss.

No electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 6. Data Retention

Personal information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant institutional, administrative, contractual or legal purpose, subject to applicable retention requirements.

## 7. Cookies

The website may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, improve user experience and understand website usage.

Users may manage cookie settings through their browser. Where applicable, additional consent choices will be provided through the website.

## 8. Third-Party Websites

BECCI may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Users should review those policies before sharing personal information.

## 9. Privacy Requests

For privacy-related questions, correction requests or concerns about personal information, contact:

**[grievance@becci.in](mailto:grievance@becci.in)**

## 10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the website, institutional activities or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version will be published on the BECCI website.
