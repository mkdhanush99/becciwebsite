# BECCI Public Website — Brief

Bahujan Entrepreneurs Chamber for Commerce & Industry. Source documents (not in repo; supplied by client):
- **PRD** = *Project requirements Document.docx* (sections §1–§28)
- **CR** = *Website Critique Review.docx* (sections §1–§21 + closing "BECCI Story")

## 1. Scope
Build the **public website only** (PRD §5, §14.1; CR public directions). Every public route, resource, opportunity listing and detail page works **without login**. English only (CR §2). Old preview (`preview.becci.in`) is background only — no visual, code, navigation or animation reuse.

**Out of scope:** membership application, OTP, KYC/caste/KYB verification (PRD §3–4, CR §7.2), payments (PRD §19), member portal/dashboards/directory (PRD §7–8), Biz Connect workflows (CR §9, PRD §9–10), Marketplace transactions (CR §10, PRD §13), referral/invitation systems (CR §7.1, PRD §11), personalised/AI recommendations (CR §11.1, PRD §14.2–14.4), admin backend (PRD §20), native apps (PRD §24), deployment/domain/hosting changes. Join Us / Member Login link to approved destinations or show honest availability + contact path. Never simulate signup, login or payment.

## 2. Identity (CR §1, §3, §4.1)
- Who: OBC-exclusive national Chamber of Commerce for Bahujan entrepreneurship, networking, enterprise development and economic opportunity. Initial operations Telangana & Andhra Pradesh, national ambition (CR §21).
- Membership basis: caste/community in Central OBC list for the State **or** a State BC/OBC list; the two are separate classifications (PRD §2). Membership eligibility ≠ scheme eligibility (PRD §2.3 last line).
- Brand promise: **Building Businesses • Building Bharat**.
- Hero headline: **Where Community Meets Commerce**; supporting line verbatim in CR §4.1.
- Must convey Identity, Value, Opportunity immediately (CR §1). Four visitor questions (CR §20).
- Copy: natural, human, distinct per page, no duplication (CR §2).
- Palette: Institutional Blue, Gold, supporting neutrals, systematic everywhere (CR §3.2).
- Typeface: client's previously named font (CR §3.1) — **name not supplied**.
- Signature: infinity symbol becoming two facing Cs (Community + Commerce); use in loader, hero, dividers, banners, selected icons, storytelling; preserve proportions (CR §3.3).
- Custom cursor where appropriate; clear hover feedback (CR §3.4) — must stay usable on keyboard/touch.
- Hero animation: luminous particle → circular motion → horizontal infinity, attracting more particles (CR §4.1). Content usable immediately; reduced-motion static version.

## 3. Five-letter story (CR closing section — use exact titles/statements)
B "The Beginning" · E "From Ideas to Enterprises" · C "Strength in Collaboration" (Community Collaboration) · C "Connecting Community with Commerce" (Commerce) · I "Creating Lasting Impact". Rules: one continuous story; hover/focus reveals letter+title+statement, previous tile resets; consistent dimensions; two Cs distinguishable with infinity linking them; tap on touch; keyboard operable with visible active state; reduced motion honoured.

## 5. Opportunity rules
Categories: Tenders/procurement; Jobs, internships, apprenticeships, student opportunities; Scholarships; Loans, grants, subsidies; Central & State schemes. Filters per master prompt §8; scheme filters CR §11 (National/State, State & District, Central/State OBC, activity, enterprise category, profile, eligibility, assistance, subsidies, deadlines).
Each record shows (as applicable): official title, authority, geography; what it provides/requires; who can apply; OBC/BC-exclusive vs documented preference vs open; Central OBC vs specific State BC/OBC rule; opening/publication/amendment/closing dates ("No deadline published" when absent); documents/registrations; official source link; official application/bid link; last checked/verified. Statuses: rolling, upcoming, open, amended, closed — colour + text. Latest/Closing Soon from real dates. Distinguish publication date, last fetch, last verification. Use official wording; no eligibility/funding guarantees (PRD §14.4). No claimed government integrations (PRD §14.7, §25). Never invent records; demo fixtures labelled and separate.

## 6. Conflicts and resolutions
1. **Navigation** — PRD §5.1 (11 items) vs CR §19 (16 items). → Grouped navigation covering both sets; Join as a Member + Member Login always visible (CR §4.2, §19; master prompt §7).
2. **Marketplace/Biz Connect** — CR §9–10 full platforms vs PRD §13 member-only and master scope. → Public explainer pages with honest availability.
3. **Membership page extras** — CR §7 Directory, Upgrade, Referral. → Describe as member features not yet available; no simulated function.
4. **Leadership contacts** — CR §6 "contact links" vs PRD §5.4 no phones. → Professional links + official organisational contact only.
5. **Opportunity breadth** — CR §12/PRD scope vs master prompt adding scholarships/jobs. → Master prompt governs; coverage limited to verified official sources, recorded in `SOURCES.md`.
6. **Membership-tier access to scheme search** (CR §7.3, §11.1). → Public browsing/search open to all (PRD §14.1); tiers mentioned only as future member features.
7. **Auto-scrolling advisors** (CR §4.3). → Provide pause control and reduced-motion fallback.
8. **Language** — PRD §14.6 multilingual future vs CR §2. → English only.
